Our Story & Ethos

Designing Spaces with Intention, Warmth & Soul

Haven Interiors was founded in Koregaon Park, Pune, with a singular belief: a home should not be an ostentatious showroom, but an intimate sanctuary where light, proportions, and natural materials bring profound daily calm.

Over the last decade, we have grown from a boutique architectural atelier into an acclaimed turnkey interior design studio, shaping over 150 signature residences, penthouses, and bespoke workspaces throughout Maharashtra.

Founder & Principal Architect

Aarushi Mehta

B.Arch (CEPT University), M.Int.Arch (London)

"We don't design to follow transient Pinterest trends. We curate spaces where when evening falls and the golden light hits your walls, you exhale and feel completely at peace. That is what turning a space into a Haven truly means."

With over 12 years of architectural training spanning London and India, Aarushi established Haven Interiors to bridge the gap between high-concept architecture and white-glove turnkey site execution. Her work has been published in Architectural Digest India, Elle Décor, and Trends Magazine.

Our Proven Methodology

The Three Pillars of Our Craft

From the first coffee conversation to the moment you unlock your front door, our methodology eliminates friction and elevates elegance.

01

Discover

We study the invisible fundamentals: how your family uses space, morning and evening solar angles, storage inventories, acoustic needs, and sensory rituals.

  • ✓ Laser site measurement & civil survey
  • ✓ Structural flow & space optimization

Design

Authentic Materiality

Materials That Age With Grace

We avoid synthetic imitations and disposable shortcuts. At Haven Interiors, we curate honest, tactile, and sustainable materials that develop rich patinas over decades:

✦

Sustainably Sourced Hardwoods

FSC-certified French oak, walnut, and seasoned Burma teak treated with eco-friendly natural oils.

✦

Honed Natural Stones

Hand-selected Italian Calacatta, Carrara, and Navona Travertine with non-reflective honed finishes.

✦

Aged Brass & Living Metals

Unlacquered brass, gunmetal, and hand-cast bronze hardware that warms with touch and time.
