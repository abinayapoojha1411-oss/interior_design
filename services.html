Client Advisory

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to help you plan your interior journey with Haven Interiors.

What is the typical timeline for a complete residential interior?

For a 3BHK to 4BHK luxury residence (2,000 to 4,500 sq.ft), the turnkey execution period typically spans 75 to 110 working days from the approval of 3D designs. We provide a milestone-locked project schedule with weekly tracking updates.

What budget ranges do you typically work with?

Our bespoke full-home turnkey projects typically start from ₹20 Lakhs for specialized room transformations, and range from ₹35 Lakhs to ₹1.5+ Crores for high-end luxury residences and villas, depending on custom joinery, imported stones, and automation specifications.

Do you undertake projects outside of Pune?

Yes! While our flagship design studio and workshop are in Koregaon Park, Pune, we regularly undertake premier residential commissions across Mumbai (Worli, Bandra, Juhu), Alibaug, Lonavala, and Goa.

What warranties and after-care do you provide?

We offer up to a 10-year manufacturer warranty on all factory-pressed modular cabinets and hardware (Blum, Hafele), alongside a 1-year complimentary studio maintenance warranty for post-handover adjustments.
